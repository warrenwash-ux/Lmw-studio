30-Day Sync Submission Cycle
One batch per month compounds quickly. Each week has one focus. Do not skip ahead.
WEEK 1
BUILD
Choose one style and build a 5 to 10 track micro-catalogue. One defined sound executed well. No outreach this week — focus entirely on craft.
WEEK 2
FINISH
Complete all versions — Full Mix, Instrumental, 60s, 30s minimum. Render all versions. Name files consistently. Organise into one shareable folder or playlist.
WEEK 3
PREPARE
Select 10 to 15 libraries that match your style. Research each briefly — listen to their catalogue. Draft your pitch using the Pitch Generator. Personalise one line per library.
WEEK 4
SUBMIT
Send submissions. Log every response in the Submission Tracker. Silence is data. Begin planning the next micro-catalogue in parallel.
